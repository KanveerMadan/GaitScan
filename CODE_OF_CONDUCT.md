# Code of conduct

GaitScan follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards
Everyone taking part in this project (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions) is expected to:
- be respectful and patient, whatever someone's experience, background or identity;
- give and take feedback on the work, not the person;
- be honest about what a measurement can and can't show, and never present it as a diagnosis;
- respect privacy: never post videos, measurements or personal details of a real person.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, publishing
someone's private information, and sustained disruption.

## Enforcement
Report problems to the maintainer, [@KanveerMadan](https://github.com/KanveerMadan), using the contact
on that profile. Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions
and may block anyone who breaks this code, temporarily or permanently, depending on how serious the
breach is.
